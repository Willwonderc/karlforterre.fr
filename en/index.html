About

The author behind the words

Passionate about the power of words and the beauty of the French language, I devote my pen to creating works that resonate with my readers’ souls.

My path has led me to explore many forms of writing, from literary fiction to professional writing, always with the same care for detail and stylistic elegance.

Every project is a new adventure, a chance to bring ideas to life and to weave stories that leave a lasting impression.

Société des Éditions du Poitou

Here is the logo I designed for the Société des Éditions du Poitou, followed by advertisements and internal documents. Also shown are the covers I created for this publishing house based in Poitiers.

The Cheminement collection

Cheminement is a collection of the Société des Éditions du Poitou. Its logo suggests momentum through its triangle, which forms a small red bookmark whose colour recalls the publisher’s social commitment.

F-Tournament

A promotional document for the F-Tournament, whose communication visuals I was entrusted with. This e-sports event encouraged bone marrow donation and won the University of Poitiers Solidarity Prize in 2021.

Artefact

Created as part of university coursework, this photomontage is based on Nicolas Boudier’s photograph for Joris Mathieu’s “Artefact”.

Le Plan

Two posters for a short film directed by Pierre Jeandroz for the Troyes festival: a humorous work with a light touch.

A typeface

My interest in typefaces and in the work of certain type foundries led me to try designing a typeface and its associated fonts.

Covers, logos and materials

Academic research

Authorship research

How does authorial mediation influence the audience of an author’s website? To answer this question, this master’s thesis in Books and Mediation draws on a questionnaire completed by 102 visitors to author websites and on interviews with people involved in creating them.

Subject

The influence of authorial mediation through the author’s website
